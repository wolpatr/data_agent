- **Risk rails & kill switches, v0.1** (2026-11-09, grilling + research): base **stoploss `-0.05`** (negative fee-inclusive ratio; `0` is a hard config error) — **bot-managed**, since Bybit spot offers no exchange-side stoploss (futures-only) and dry-run simulates everything anyway; **no trailing stop** in v0.1 (defers to the v0.2 model phase). Three protections declared **in the strategy file** (config-level `"protections"` was removed in 2024.10 — hard `ConfigurationError`), starting values in 1h candle units, every knob to be swept in backtests: `CooldownPeriod` locks re-entry for 2 candles after any close; `StoplossGuard` locks entries 4 candles after ≥2 losing stoploss-family exits in 24 candles; `MaxDrawdown` (global-only, realized/closed-trade drawdown) locks all entries 24 candles after ≥20% drawdown over 168 candles with ≥5 trades armed — `calculation_mode` set to docs-recommended **`equity`** (amended same day, second research scan, from the `ratios`-default plan: equivalent metrics under a fixed stake and the stable docs recommend it; revisit in the sweep). Protections create DB-persisted locks that **block new entries only, never exits**, and survive restarts; with `max_open_trades: 1` an open loser is bounded only by the stoploss (all protections are realized-only). **External kill = process stop** (Ctrl+C/SIGTERM same path) with `cancel_open_orders_on_exit: true` — unfilled orders cancelled, open positions never force-exited; after a restart the bot boots idle (`initial_state` unset → STOPPED) until a human `/start` re-arms it. **Manual-intervention channel = freqtrade REST API on localhost only** (8080, credentials in `config-private.json`; freqUI/Telegram decision stands separately): `/stopentry` (aliases `/pause`, `/stopbuy`; blocks entries while exits stay managed — in-memory, resets on restart), `/stop` (full halt), `/forceexit`, `/locks` (GET/POST/DELETE); there is **no `/stopexit`**. **Policy**: the human may pause entries, force-exit, full-stop, and add locks; may **not** force-enter trades or edit the dry-run trade DB (attribution must stay clean); interventions are logged for Evaluate. Sources: [risk-rails fact-finding](../../research/freqtrade-risk-rails.md), freqtrade stable docs — [stoploss](https://www.freqtrade.io/en/stable/stoploss/), [plugins/protections](https://www.freqtrade.io/en/stable/plugins/#protections), [rest-api](https://www.freqtrade.io/en/stable/rest-api/), [configuration](https://www.freqtrade.io/en/stable/configuration/).
- **v0.1 trigger strategy: EMA 12/26 crossover baseline** (2026-11-09, grilling + research): freqtrade strategy class **`EmaCrossBaseline`** (interface v3) — **enter** on the golden cross (fast EMA12 crossing above slow EMA26 on the closed 1h candle, executed at the next candle open), **exit exclusively on the death cross**; `minimal_roi = {}` (docs-documented ROI disable, superseding the legacy `{"0": 100}` convention floated in the grill), `can_short = False` (spot long-only), `process_only_new_candles = True`, `startup_candle_count: 100` (~4× the slow EMA per docs ratio; confirm with `freqtrade recursive-analysis` pre-dry-run) — carrying the decided rails in-file (`stoploss = -0.05` plus the three protections as a strategy `@property`). Period pair pinned **12/26** (grounded in TA-Lib's MACD 12/26 conventions; 9/21 and 50/200 verified as ungrounded community lore; repo example Strategy001 uses 20/50 but with a deliberately *asymmetric* exit — our symmetric death-cross bookends are the chosen design, and freqtrade-strategies' own README marks its strategies "starting points"). Period comparison happens **only in the backtest sweep**, never as parallel dry-runs. Framing (citation-backed): this baseline exists to exercise the infrastructure, not to claim edge — technical/MA rules are the field's canonical baseline layer (Fang et al. survey §3.2/§6.1), and both ML papers (2606.00060, 2407.18334) situate their contributions in the model phase that replaces this signal in v0.2 via the same `enter_long`/`exit_long` seam without touching rails or config. Sources: [trigger-baseline research](../../research/trigger-strategy-baseline.md), [strategy-customization docs](https://www.freqtrade.io/en/stable/strategy-customization/).
- **Evaluate & reporting: freqUI on the local REST API** (2026-11-09, grilling): the **dry-run SQLite DB + trade export stays the baseline record**; the human-facing channel is **freqUI** served by the already-approved localhost REST API (one `freqtrade install-ui` command, no new credentials); **no Telegram in v0.1** — deferred to fog (bot signup + notification surface; revisit alongside the VPS-ops item).
